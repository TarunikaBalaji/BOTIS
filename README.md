## 🚨 The Problem

Organ transplantation is one of the most time-sensitive procedures in modern medicine.

Once an organ is retrieved, its viability starts declining immediately. Traffic congestion, fragmented communication, and manual coordination can delay transport and reduce the probability of a successful transplant.

### Current Challenges

- 🚦 Traffic congestion causes critical delays.
- 📞 Green Corridor activation is largely manual and reactive.
- 🏥 Hospitals lack a unified real-time monitoring system.
- 🚑 Ambulance drivers do not receive AI-guided emergency routing.
- 👮 Traffic police lack contextual, actionable information.
- ⏱ Every minute of delay reduces organ survival probability.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

> How can we build an intelligent coordination platform that enables hospitals, ambulance drivers, and traffic police to collaboratively reduce organ transport delays and maximize organ survival probability?

---

## 💡 Our Solution

BOTIS (**B**OTIS — **O**rgan **T**ransport **I**ntelligence **S**ystem) is an AI-powered platform that acts as a digital coordination layer for emergency organ transport.

It combines:

- 🖥️ **Transport Intelligence Dashboard** for transplant coordinators
- 📱 **Mobile App** for ambulance drivers and traffic police
- 🧠 **AI-powered delay prediction and route optimization**
- 🚦 **Green Corridor recommendation and activation support**
- 📊 **Survival impact analytics**

BOTIS does not replace existing emergency systems—it augments them with real-time intelligence and coordinated decision support.

---

## ✨ Key Innovations

### 🧠 AI-Driven Decision Support
Continuously analyzes transport conditions and recommends interventions before delays become critical.

### 🚦 Intelligent Green Corridor Coordination
Predicts when a Green Corridor should be activated and quantifies its benefit.

### ⏱ Organ Survival Monitoring
Displays a live viability timer and risk indicators.

### 👥 Multi-Stakeholder Coordination
Connects hospitals, drivers, and traffic police in one unified ecosystem.

### 📈 Impact Visualization
Clearly shows measurable outcomes:

- **ETA:** `47 min → 28 min`
- **Survival Probability:** `78% → 94%`
- **Risk Level:** `HIGH → LOW`
